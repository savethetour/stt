Background & Context

The 16 November 2026 Decision

Our Music department is fully behind touring. The decision not to proceed in Summer 2027 was taken at School Administration level, which is why our appeal is addressed respectfully to the Headmaster and Senior Leadership.

16 Nov 2026

Why the Tour Was Deferred to 2028

In his letter to families on 16 November 2026, the Head Mr. Windsor confirmed that despite a healthy level of parental and pupil interest, the proposed Summer 2027 Germany tour would not proceed. School Leadership has moved most multi-year-group trips to a biennial cycle, proposing the next overseas Music tour for Summer 2028 in order to balance educational visits across school activities and ease organisational workload.

Proposal

Start the Biennial Cycle After 2027

Students and families genuinely appreciate the School's care for staff wellbeing and trip fairness. However, because expressions of interest for Germany 2027 had already been gathered, and because current Upper Sixth Form musicians will have left Abingdon by Summer 2028, we ask Leadership to honour the 2027 tour and run the biennial cycle from 2027 to 2029 instead.

The Arguments

Why Reinstating Summer 2027 Matters

01

Current Senior Pupils Will Miss Their Only Overseas Tour

Pupils in the Upper Sixth have rehearsed weekly in Symphony Orchestra, Chapel Choir, and Big Band. Deferring the tour from 2027 to 2028 means some senior leaders, section principals leave Abingdon without ever representing the School musically abroad.

02

A Fair Biennial Compromise: 2027 → 2029

We fully support a sustainable biennial rhythm for overseas trips. Honoring the already-announced Summer 2027 Germany tour, where healthy family demand is already proven; and scheduling the following overseas Music tour for Summer 2029 preserves the exact two-year cycle without penalising the cohort that signed up in good faith.

03

Easing Organiser Workload Through Practical Support

Whilst the Music department is in charge of concerts, external specialist tour operators handle transport, venues, accommodation, and risk-assessment logistics, and Sixth Form pupils can assist with repertoire packs, stage management, and tour administration under staff supervision.

The Ensembles

Symphony Orchestra, Chapel Choir & Big Band

Bringing all three flagship ensembles together allows Abingdon to perform symphonic masterworks in German concert halls, sacred choral repertoire in historic cathedrals, and high-energy big band sets in open-air town squares.

Symphony Orchestra

Performing Robert Schumann's Symphony No. 3 ("Rhenish") in the very Rhineland cities that inspired its five movements.

Chapel Choir

Singing with the orchestra for Fauré's Requiem, alongside unaccompanied Evensong services in Rhineland cathedrals.

Big Band

Bringing funk and swing energy to German summer festivals with signature pieces.

Why is this petition restricted to @abingdon.org.uk accounts? +

Senior Leadership makes trip decisions based on genuine internal demand from current pupils and fee-paying families. Restricting signatures to verified Google accounts guarantees zero duplicate or outside entries.

How can parents and OAs support the campaign? +

Parents and OAs can use Template 2 above to send a polite, direct email or printed letter to Mr Mike Windsor's office (sophie.willis@abingdon.org.uk) expressing their support for keeping Summer 2027 in place before beginning the biennial cycle.

Is this campaign respectful of our Music teachers and School Leadership? +

Yes. Our Music staff organise nearly 90 concerts a year and wholeheartedly support pupil music-making. This petition is a polite, constructive student request asking Senior Leadership to honour the 2027 cohort before moving to a 2027 → 2029 biennial schedule.
