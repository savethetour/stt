Background & Context

The 16 November 2026 School Administration Decision

A factual overview of why this petition exists—and why Abingdon’s student musicians are standing alongside our Music Department to appeal an administrative scheduling decision.

CORRESPONDENCE · 16 NOVEMBER 2026 From Headmaster Mr Mike Windsor

Earlier in the academic year, expressions of interest were gathered for a Summer 2027 Music Department tour to Germany—referenced in the Headmaster’s correspondence for the Symphony Orchestra and Big Band alongside our wider senior touring cohort uniting Symphony Orchestra, Chapel Choir, and Big Band. A healthy number of families expressed interest, and our Music Department is ready and eager to lead the tour.

However, on 16 November 2026, Headmaster Mr Mike Windsor wrote to families announcing that Senior Leadership had decided the Summer 2027 tour will not proceed. Administration cited a policy to move most multi-year-group trips to a biennial cycle (deferring the next overseas Music tour to Summer 2028 to spread trips fairly across all school activities and ease organiser workload) while acknowledging that the Music Department staged nearly 90 concerts last year.

Crucially, our Music Department supports the tour—the cancellation was a Senior Leadership scheduling decision that disproportionately penalises current Upper Sixth and senior musicians across all three ensembles who will leave Abingdon before Summer 2028.

Key Distinction

Our Music Department supports the tour. Senior Leadership made the cancellation decision.

  • 1 Strong Family Demand: Healthy expressions of interest were already submitted for Summer 2027.
  • 2 Thriving Department: Music staff delivered ~90 concerts last year and are committed to giving pupils international performance opportunities.
  • 3 Constructive Appeal: Every signature and formal letter on savethetour.de is addressed respectfully to Senior Leadership to ask for a 2027 → 2029 biennial transition.
Three-Point Appeal

The Case for Summer 2027

A practical compromise that honours the School’s biennial trip framework without depriving graduating student musicians of their final overseas tour.

01

Upper Sixth Leavers Lose Their Final Tour

Upper Sixth players in Symphony Orchestra and Big Band, together with senior choristers in Chapel Choir, lose their final opportunity to tour internationally together if the tour is deferred to Summer 2028—after they have already left Abingdon.

02

Biennial Cycle Starting 2027 → 2029

Running Summer 2027—for which healthy expressions of interest are already in place—and establishing the two-year cycle from 2027 to 2029 preserves fairness across all school activities without penalising current leavers.

03

Supporting Our Thriving Music Staff

Our Music Department delivered nearly 90 concerts last year and wants this tour to succeed. Senior musicians, music prefects, and parent volunteers are ready to assist with rehearsal administration and section logistics to keep staff workload manageable.
