Background & Context

The 16 November 2026 Decision

The decision not to proceed in Summer 2027 was taken at School Administration level, which is why our appeal is addressed respectfully to the Head and Senior Leadership.

16 November 2026

Why the Tour Was Deferred to 2028

In his letter to families on 16 November 2026, the Head, Mr Windsor, confirmed that despite a healthy level of parental and pupil interest, the proposed Summer 2027 Germany tour would not proceed. School Leadership has moved most multi-year-group trips to a biennial cycle, proposing the next overseas Music tour for Summer 2028 in order to balance educational visits across school activities and ease organisational workload.

Our Proposal

Start the Biennial Cycle After 2027

We appreciate the School's aim of balancing trips and protecting staff workload. However, because expressions of interest for Germany 2027 had already been gathered, and because current Upper Sixth Form musicians will have left Abingdon by Summer 2028, we ask Leadership to honour the 2027 tour and run the biennial cycle from 2027 to 2029 instead.

The Case

Why 2027, Not 2028

01

Current Senior & Exam-Year Pupils Will Miss Out

Pupils across the Upper Sixth, Lower Sixth, and Fifth Year rehearse weekly in Symphony Orchestra, Chapel Choir, and Big Band. Deferring the tour from 2027 to 2028 means Upper Sixth section principals and choir leads leave Abingdon without ever representing the School musically abroad, while current Fourth Years face GCSE public examinations in Summer 2028.

02

A Fair Biennial Compromise: 2027 → 2029

We fully support a sustainable biennial rhythm for overseas trips. Honouring the already-announced Summer 2027 Germany tour, where healthy family demand is already proven, and scheduling the following overseas Music tour for Summer 2029 preserves the exact two-year cycle without penalising the cohort that signed up in good faith.

03

Easing Organiser Workload Through Practical Support

Whilst the Music department is in charge of concerts, external specialist tour operators handle transport, venues, accommodation, and risk-assessment logistics, and Sixth Form pupils can assist with repertoire packs, stage management, and tour administration under staff supervision.

The Ensembles

Symphony Orchestra, Chapel Choir & Big Band

Bringing all three flagship ensembles together allows Abingdon to perform symphonic masterworks in German concert halls, sacred choral repertoire in historic cathedrals, and high-energy big band sets in open-air town squares.

Performing Schumann's Symphony No. 3 ("Rhenish") in the Rhineland cities that inspired its five movements.

Chapel Choir

Singing Fauré's Requiem, alongside unaccompanied Evensong services in Rhineland cathedrals.

Big Band

Playing Pee Wee Ellis's "The Chicken", Nina Simone's "Feeling Good", and other set favourites at summer concerts.

Why is the petition restricted to school accounts? +

Senior Leadership makes trip decisions based on genuine internal demand from current pupils and fee-paying families. Restricting signatures to verified Google accounts guarantees zero duplicate or outside entries.

How can parents and OAs support the campaign? +

Parents and OAs can send a polite, direct email to Mr Mike Windsor's office expressing their support for keeping Summer 2027 in place before beginning the biennial cycle.

Is this campaign respectful of our Music teachers and School Leadership? +

Yes. Our Music staff organise nearly 90 concerts a year and wholeheartedly support pupil music-making. This petition is a polite, constructive student request asking Senior Leadership to honour the 2027 cohort before moving to a 2027 → 2029 biennial schedule.

How is the Germany tour funded, and is there enough family demand? +

Overseas Music tours are self-funded by participating families and coordinated through an external specialist educational tour operator. As the Head noted in his 16 November 2026 letter, a healthy level of parental and pupil interest had already been registered before the tour was deferred.
